# Keystone prototype

An Abu Dhabi relocation walkthrough that turns a checklist into an order of actions. The user-approved KEYSTONE_SPEC.md pins a monument-inspired landing page and bilingual workspace with one next action, five dependency lanes, document review, housing cash planning and blocker recovery.

This milestone is a local visual prototype. People, extracts, costs, dates and city cohorts are synthetic. No personal files are uploaded, applications submitted or AI integrations represented as live. Government requirements need independent review before production.
